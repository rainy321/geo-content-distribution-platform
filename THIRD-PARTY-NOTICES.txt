Playwright and Patchright driver license and third-party notice files are
included inside their packaged driver directories. This file identifies the
other packages above but does not assert that their full license texts are
embedded by PyInstaller. The executable uses an independently installed Google
Chrome browser for publishing; Chrome is not redistributed in this release.
Authorized-real builds embed the pinned Node.js runtime and official Weibo CLI.
Their package and runtime license files remain inside the extracted executable
payload, and their exact versions and hashes/integrity are recorded in the
release manifest. Weibo account authorization, developer verification, service
activation, Credits and platform limits remain the operator's responsibility.

Bilibili's biliup executable is not included, downloaded, updated, or
redistributed by the desktop package.  An authorized-real build can recognize
one external compatibility-runtime version only after an exact executable
size, SHA-256, and offline version probe all pass.  The safe profile never
enables it.  This compatibility check is not a license grant and does not
authorize downloading, operating for commercial customers, or redistributing
biliup; those rights and Bilibili platform authorization require separate
review.  The preferred commercial integration path is Bilibili's official
Open Platform with approved application credentials.

The colleague handoff archive used as a reference for the AI visibility port
did not contain its own LICENSE or NOTICE. This inventory does not cure that
missing rights statement. Until written authorization or a verifiable license
is supplied, builds containing that port remain limited to the distribution
scope stated in GEO-Content-Platform.manifest.json (Windows binary delivery) or
SOURCE-MANIFEST.json (source delivery).

This file is an engineering inventory, not a legal opinion or a complete
redistribution notice. The project owner must generate a complete dependency
inventory and finish license and trademark review before external commercial
distribution.
